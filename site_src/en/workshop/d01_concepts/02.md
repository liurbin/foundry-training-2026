# D1-02: Complete the Foundry Fit Decision Card

> Module: D1 Concepts and Decision Framework | Credentials: None

## Purpose
Fill the decision card and make a clear call. This step turns the module decision into a concrete learner artifact that can be reviewed during class.

## Instructions
- Copy the relevant decision-card template from the spec library.
- Fill every required field; do not leave maybe or TBD as the conclusion.
- Write one reason for the selected path and one reason for each rejected path.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The requested artifact exists and can be reviewed by the instructor or assistant.
- The artifact uses concrete values from your project rather than generic placeholders.
- Any uncertainty is written explicitly instead of hidden.
- The output remains consistent with earlier decision cards unless you document why the decision changed.

## Submission
- Save the artifact in the matching `submissions/` folder for this step.
- Include short notes, screenshots, logs, or trace links only when they prove the acceptance criteria.

## Common Mistakes
- Choosing Foundry only because the customer uses Azure.
- Comparing a managed platform and an orchestration library as if they were the same category.
- Leaving the conclusion as maybe or it depends.
