# D1: Concepts and Decision Framework

> Module type: discussion / decision | Duration: 45 min | Credentials: None

## Goals
- Place your own project on the Foundry capability map.
- Reach a clear use / do not use / partially use conclusion.
- Name common cases where Foundry is the wrong choice.
- Explain at least one comparison against LangGraph or direct Azure OpenAI.

## Prerequisites
- Bring a real or planned agent-style project and describe its business goal in one sentence.
- Skim the Foundry capability map before class.

## Subtasks
1. [Foundry Capability Map Overview](01.md) - Mark each capability domain as used, not used, or uncertain.
2. [Complete the Foundry Fit Decision Card](02.md) - Fill the decision card and make a clear call.
3. [Review and Negative Examples](03.md) - Check your card against common wrong assumptions.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The decision card contains at least one Foundry-positive and one Foundry-negative signal, or explains why one side does not apply.
- The conclusion is explicit: use, do not use, or partially use.
- Uncertainties are written down instead of hidden.

## Credential Notes
None.

## Common Mistakes
- Choosing Foundry only because the customer uses Azure.
- Comparing a managed platform and an orchestration library as if they were the same category.
- Leaving the conclusion as maybe or it depends.
